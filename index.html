About us

Embracing the digital media revolution.

Media View Africa is a digital media services, public relations and communication consultancy based in Kenya.

We work where journalism, communication and technology meet. Our newsroom publishes news, analysis and opinion across politics, business, agriculture and society. Our consultancy helps organisations tell their own stories with the same discipline: verify first, write plainly, publish where people actually read.

Audiences across Africa now find information on their phones, in their feeds and in their messages. We build content, campaigns and communication strategies for that reality.

We are also on location. From boardrooms and product launches to weddings and community celebrations, our crews cover events as they happen and publish the story while it is still fresh.

Website proposal for Media View Africa

This page is a working prototype of the proposed site. Copy and sample stories are drafts for the team to confirm.

Site map

  • Home: live-desk ticker, latest stories, services, beats
  • About: story, principles, brand
  • Our Services: Digital Media, Events & Media Coverage, PR, Consultancy and a brief builder
  • Newsroom: categories, search, reader, publishing desk
  • Contact Us: details and enquiry form

Newsroom workflow

  • Roles: reporter, editor, administrator
  • Draft, review and publish states with scheduled posts
  • Categories: Politics, Business & Economy, Agriculture, Social & Society, Tech, Sports, Environment
  • Story types: news update, blog, opinion, feature
  • Images, video embeds, author profiles and tags

Recommended build

  • Fast static front end with a headless CMS for the newsroom
  • Edge hosting and image optimisation for low-bandwidth readers
  • SEO, Open Graph cards and Google News-ready structure
  • Newsletter sign-up, analytics and a Kiswahili option
  • Accessibility checks and mobile-first testing

Delivery phases

  • Discover: goals, audience, content audit
  • Design: brand system and page designs
  • Build: front end, CMS, newsroom roles
  • Launch: training, migration, go-live support

In this prototype

  • Publishing works for signed-in staff with edit access; stories are shared live
  • The contact form prepares a brief to send by email or phone; automated delivery comes with the production build
